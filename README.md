# Pop-Up Rally Lights
A set of off-roading aux lights that come with the function to flip up like sports cars from the 80's and 90's

![image](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/db097677-5600-4287-8b94-c4583f2c20cc)

All of the parts have been designed with 3D printing in mind, and very few of the pieces require support material. Everything should be printed in ABS or stronger filament, and critical components such as the pillars that hold up the lights should be wrapped in either fiberglass or carbon fiber to reduce the chances of it snapping off and flying into oncoming traffic.

## Neccesary materials
- ABS or stronger filament
- 12V electric scooter motor
- Gear/Sprocket Set
- Arduino/ESP/RPi microcontroller
- H-Bridge motor controller
- Switches
- 4 circular aux lights of your choosing
- Wires n stuff
- Choose 1:
  - 1 Piece 2040 extrusion (1220mm)
  - 2 pieces 2020 extrusion (1220mm)
- Choose 1:
  - Fiberglass + epoxy
  - Carbon fiber + epoxy
